## Member experience

- Guests see only sign-in and account creation. A signed-in Firebase account can enter without a verification email.
- A required five-step popup collects name, optional photo, skills, languages, cryosphere interests, career stage, education, gender, motivation, contributions, availability and consent. Existing profiles receive the questionnaire once to complete the new fields.
- Every selection question offers an Add option field. New choices are saved in a shared Firestore catalog and appear for subsequent registrants. Selected personal answers are separate from this catalog.
- Gender, sentiment and feedback are private: only the member and full UNESCO Secretariat administrators can read them. Gender defaults to Prefer not to say. Career stage and education offer the same opt-out.
- Members can edit profiles, search the directory, follow Working Groups, publish updates, propose activities and record contributions. Profiles can be community-visible or private; contact email requires separate consent.
- PNG, JPEG and WebP profile photos are cropped to a 256-pixel square and compressed to JPEG. Photos are kept in separate Firestore documents, limited to 100,000 characters each. Photo visibility follows profile visibility.

## Refresh and account access

Community records refresh every 60 seconds in an active online browser tab. Refresh is paused during saves, profile editing, onboarding and open dialogs to preserve unfinished inputs. It updates data without reloading the page and skips overlapping requests. Manual Refresh remains available. Role removal updates navigation on the next data refresh; Firestore access restrictions apply immediately.

Automatic refresh reuses bounded queries and consumes Firestore reads. It is a small-community implementation, not unlimited capacity. Hidden tabs do not poll. Category options retain their live listener.

Registration and existing-account sign-in lead directly to the required profile questionnaire and the community. No verification email is sent, and normal community access does not depend on `emailVerified`. Firebase still stores that flag truthfully; the app does not mark unverified addresses as verified. Password reset and email-recovery links remain supported, together with links issued before this update. **EMAIL_SETUP.md** explains the remaining email operations.

The owner email bootstrap retains its identity check to prevent a new unverified account claiming the owner's email from gaining full editing. Use **Sign in with Google** as **alexandregermouty@gmail.com** for that initial access; Google sign-in requires no Firebase verification message. Other UNESCO Secretariat accounts receive access only through protected roles assigned by the Secretariat.

## Spark plan and limits

This package uses Firebase Hosting, Authentication and Standard-edition Cloud Firestore. It uses no Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, App Hosting, SMS service, payment integration or external email service, and has no billing upgrade step. Password reset emails use Firebase Authentication; registration sends no verification email.

Queries load at most 100 directory profiles, 50 open activities, 50 owned activities, 100 management activities, 25 recent updates, 50 personal contributions and 100 management contributions and 100 recent/upcoming events for WG spaces. Search and editing apply to the loaded records. The interface flags full batches; active pages refresh data once per minute outside editing flows. Shared categories use a live listener so new options reach open forms. Every photo and category record consumes Firestore reads. There is no automatic email invitation or notification service.

Spark quotas remain applicable. This is a small community pilot, without complete directory pagination or full-text search. Removing or moving a record with hundreds of linked contributions requires reducing those linked records first. Deleting member data does not delete the Authentication account; that account may create a fresh profile. Deployment retains existing profiles and contributions.

## Permissions

- Shared data requires a signed-in Firebase account. Fictional profiles, activities, updates and the example toggle have been removed.
- Members edit their own profile, private answers and photo. Private profiles and photos are visible only to their owner and UNESCO Secretariat.
- UNESCO Secretariat administrators edit platform records and grant/revoke other administrators. Normal members cannot self-promote.
- Member activity proposals begin pending. Scoped WG managers can publish/close activities and review contributions in their groups.
- WG managers cannot change contribution identities or reverse a member withdrawal. UNESCO Secretariat can correct records and statuses.
- Firestore validates fields, sizes, string lists, ownership, roles and timestamps. Shared gender categories contain labels, never a person's selected private answer.

## Calendar and public resources (3.4)

Calendar displays six weeks with day selection, previous/next month, Today, and OC/MG filters. Every signed-in member can view events. Members with a completed profile can add events and edit/delete their own entries. Only UNESCO Secretariat can edit/delete another member’s event. Event ownership and creation time remain fixed.

Events store UTC timestamps with the organiser’s IANA time zone. Display follows the browser/device time zone, refreshed every minute and on return to the page. It does not use IP geolocation. New events use the device time zone; editing retains the organiser’s original zone, shown on the form. Nonexistent daylight-saving clock times are rejected; repeated hours require choosing an occurrence. Events may last up to 31 days. A midnight ending excludes the following day.

Each event includes a title, optional description/location, registration link, virtual meeting link, at least one OC1–5 and one MG1.1–5.3. Selecting a main goal selects its parent challenge. Removing a challenge clears its goals. Links accept HTTP/HTTPS only. Events are visible to the signed-in community; the form does not claim external approval or register visitors automatically.

Two bounded Firestore listeners track start/end timestamps around the visible month. They update the page without reload and unsubscribe on navigation or a hidden/offline tab. Each query displays up to 200 records and reads a 201st record to detect a full batch. A notice appears if results may be incomplete. Spark quotas apply; no Functions, Storage, paid geolocation or billing change is needed. No composite index is required for the calendar. Deleting a member’s data removes their events with their linked community records.

The Official Decade website tab offers two links for every signed-in member:
- **Publish your content on the official website of the Decade**: https://www.un-cryosphere.org/en/get-involved/share
- **Apply to carry the logo of the Decade**: https://www.un-cryosphere.org/en/get-involved/submit-activity

They open the official forms in a new tab. The separate restricted Core Group workspace retains the logo-review platform. Submitting a calendar event does not submit either official form.
